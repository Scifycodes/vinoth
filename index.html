About Me

I am a mathematics person with a strong academic background and a deep commitment to research and higher education. My academic journey began with a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Mathematics from Sacred Heart College, Tirupattur. With a continued passion for the subject, I pursued both my Master’s degree (M.Sc.) and M.Phil. in Mathematics at Islamiah College, Vaniyambadi, where I built a solid foundation in advanced mathematical theory and analytical methodologies.

To further expand my expertise and contribute meaningfully to the field of mathematical research, I earned my Doctorate (Ph.D.) in Mathematics from Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), a globally recognized institution known for its emphasis on academic excellence and innovation.

My areas of interest lie in applied mathematics, neutrosophic sets, computer vision, image processing, and my work is driven by a pursuit of rigorous inquiry, logical precision, and impactful academic contributions.
